# Queue Management Case Study
Case study on queue management in college canteens using participatory observational research

\# Queue Management in College Canteens



This repository contains a case study titled  

\*\*"Queue Management in College Canteens: A Participatory and Observational Approach to Understanding Waiting Experience and Process Delays"\*\*



\## Overview

\- Research Type: Case Study

\- Methodology: Participatory Observational Research

\- Context: Five college canteens

\- Sample Size: 250 structured observations



\## Key Focus Areas

\- Waiting experience vs actual waiting time

\- Behavioral and emotional responses during peak and non-peak hours

\- Impact of digital order displays and process visibility

\- Service design implications for queue management
